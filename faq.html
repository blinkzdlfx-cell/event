Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services

Herkinx Events offers a comprehensive range of luxury event decoration services including wedding decoration, traditional marriage decoration, birthday decoration, proposal decoration, engagement decoration, bridal shower, baby shower, anniversary decoration, graduation parties, corporate events, church decoration, outdoor events, indoor events, reception decoration, stage decoration, table styling, floral decoration, balloon decoration, entrance decoration, luxury event styling, event planning, and event coordination.

We recommend booking our services at least 3-6 months in advance for weddings and large events, and 1-2 months for smaller events like birthday parties. However, we do our best to accommodate last-minute bookings based on availability. The earlier you book, the more time we have to plan and create the perfect decoration for your event.

Booking is easy! Simply fill out our online booking form on the Contact/Booking page with your event details. We'll review your request and contact you within 24 hours to discuss your needs and schedule a consultation. You can also reach us directly via phone or email. A deposit is required to confirm your booking.

We understand that plans can change. Our cancellation policy is as follows: Cancellations made 30+ days before the event receive a full refund minus the deposit. Cancellations made 15-29 days before receive a 50% refund. Cancellations made less than 15 days before the event are non-refundable. We recommend purchasing event insurance for unforeseen circumstances.

Our pricing varies based on the type of event, venue size, decoration complexity, and specific requirements. We offer packages starting from ₦500,000 for smaller events to ₦10,000,000+ for luxury weddings. We provide detailed quotes after our initial consultation. Every package is customizable to fit your budget and needs.

Yes, we offer flexible payment plans to make our services accessible. Typically, we require a 50% deposit to secure your booking, with the remaining balance due 7 days before the event. For larger events, we can arrange installment payments. We accept bank transfers, cash, and mobile payments.
